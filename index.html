Who actually watches

Viewers over the last 28 days — not followers accumulated over years

The content is in Russian, and the audience follows the language rather than one border. It is not the same audience on every platform, so the two largest channels are shown separately rather than averaged into a single misleading number.

The biggest channel is also the most concentrated

Facebook carries half of all reach here — 59M of 120M — and it is the most Ukraine-weighted and least Russia-weighted of the four: 56.1% Ukraine against 5.5% Russia. For an advertiser with restrictions on the Russian market, that is the channel to buy.

Georgia at 9.4%, with Tbilisi the second city after Kyiv at 17.1%, is a cluster most kits in this language would not have.

Said plainly, before you ask

On TikTok, Russia and Belarus together are close to 30% of viewers. On Facebook, Russia is 5.5%. Neither can be targeted away entirely, because distribution is algorithmic rather than bought.

If that rules out a campaign, better to know now than after a contract. Individual placements have run tighter — the three sponsored posts above reached between 73% and 94% Ukraine.
